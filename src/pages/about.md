**headlesslife** is a working notebook about running a life with no single
"head" — no one app, account, or device that everything depends on.

Topics, roughly:

- Stateless machines and reproducible setups
- Portable, plain-text data
- Small, quiet software built in the open

This site is static HTML. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking.
